Arena 100k: do humans reward the 2024 structural style?

Lab book · 2026-11-11 · frozen v19 (sha256 1f63ee85…) through reading frame r1.1 · predictions frozen in PLAN.md · structure index and units fixed from the AlpacaEval study

Findings
  • Mid-2024 human voters reward the structure, beyond length and repetition (β +0.132 [+0.111, +0.152]), at a structure-to-length ratio of +0.214, not distinguishable from MT-Bench humans in 2023 (+0.137).
  • The reward disappears between two styled models. When neither model writes in the 2024 style, the structure weight is +0.234; when both do, +0.002 [-0.057, +0.054]. This is not a narrower range: the structure difference is wider between styled models (SD 1.44 vs 1.21), and unstyled battles resampled to the same spread still give +0.224 [+0.177, +0.292].
  • Bold title lines are not rewarded in themselves (-0.038 [-0.085, +0.003]). Markdown headers are (added check: +0.333 [+0.278, +0.391]).
  • Judge vs human, in fixed units. The AlpacaEval judge's ratio (+0.377) is above Arena humans' (difference [+0.100, +0.225]). On MT-Bench's same pairs, GPT-4 (+0.151) matches humans (difference [-0.204, +0.229]). The gap therefore tracks the dataset or judge version (GPT-4-turbo on AlpacaEval's 2023-24 models vs GPT-4 on MT-Bench's 2023 models), not judges vs humans as such.

6 · Across the four studies

  • Humans reward the structure and penalise repetition in all three human datasets (HelpSteer2 within pairs, MT-Bench, Arena). In fixed units the structure-to-length ratio is +0.137 (MT-Bench) and +0.214 (Arena).
  • GPT-4 on the same pairs matches humans (MT-Bench). The higher AlpacaEval judge ratio comes with a different judge version and a 2024 model population.
  • The 2024 style is rewarded by humans up to a point. Between two unstyled models, more structure wins votes; between two styled models, it gains nothing. Beyond length, repetition and structure, bold title lines carry no reward; headers do.
