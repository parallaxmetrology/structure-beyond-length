MT-Bench: humans vs GPT-4 on the same pairs

Lab book · 2026-11-11 · frozen v19 (sha256 1f63ee85…) through reading frame r1 · predictions frozen in PLAN.md · structure index fixed from the AlpacaEval study

Findings
  • On the same pairs, GPT-4 does not detectably weight structure more than humans do, relative to length. Structure-to-length ratio: humans +0.171, GPT-4 +0.257; difference +0.085, 95% CI [-0.161, +0.345]. Majority-vote check: +0.104 [-0.066, +0.299].
  • Mostly consistent with GPT-4 being a less noisy copy of the human preference. Its weights are 1.53× the human ones on length, 1.61× on repetition and 1.58× on segmentation (rᵥ); uniform scaling is what cleaner labels alone would produce. Rare vocabulary (xₚ, 2.1×) and clause stacking (dₛ, 2.0×) sit above that line, in the predicted direction, but their intervals include 0.
  • Repetition is the largest structural signal for both. Adding it to length lifts held-out AUC from 0.686 to 0.826 (humans) and 0.739 to 0.852 (GPT-4). The kernel adds nothing held-out beyond that here.
  • Agreement reproduces the original paper's pattern: human-human 81%, human-GPT-4 81% (non-tie votes).
  • Operating window. These are 2023 models; none of the 655 eligible answers has a bold title line (0%). The formatting style that appeared in 2024 cannot be tested on this dataset.

1 · Data

lmsys/mt_bench_human_judgments (CC-BY-4.0). 80 questions × 2 turns × 6 models (alpaca-13b, claude-v1, gpt-3.5-turbo, gpt-4, llama-13b, vicuna-13b-v1.2). 960 distinct answers read, 655 eligible (excluded: short_prose 298, no_letters 22, low_english_function_words 14, non_latin_script 2). Short answers are common in MT-Bench (math, single-value replies), so about a third drop out.

Analysed: 708 pairs judged by both sides with a non-tie verdict and both answers eligible; 1228 human votes (65 judges in the full set) and 708 GPT-4 verdicts on those pairs.

5 · How this fits the earlier studies

  • HelpSteer2: within a prompt, humans reward non-repetition and a little structure beyond length.
  • AlpacaEval: the GPT-4-turbo judge rewards the same direction, with larger weights; sizes were not comparable across datasets.
  • MT-Bench (same pairs): relative to length, GPT-4's structural weighting is not distinguishable from humans'. The larger AlpacaEval weights are consistent with wider pair differences and cleaner labels rather than a judge-specific taste. The 2024 formatting style is outside this dataset's window.
