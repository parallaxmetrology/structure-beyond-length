Style-control baseline

Lab book · 2026-11-11 · no new data or compute: refits on saved votes, judge verdicts and reward-model scores · plan frozen in PLAN.md before fitting

Findings
  • For Arena voters, Arena's own style-control features (length, headers, bold, lists) carry most of the structure signal: the kernel's remaining weight is +0.072 on all battles and not detectable within either cohort.
  • For the Claude judge and the three reward models the kernel captures what the markdown counts miss, most of all between styled answers (judge +0.127; reward models +0.283 to +0.394).
  • PairRM barely responds to Arena's features (AUC 0.512 and 0.530); the kernel explains most of what it rewards.
  • The voters-versus-reward-models gap persists between styled answers and opens between unstyled ones once standard style control is applied.
