HelpSteer2 vs the v19 kernel

Lab book · 2026-11-11 · frozen v19 (sha256 1f63ee85…) through reading frame r1 · predictions frozen in PLAN.md before the first reading

Findings
  • Comparing two answers to the same prompt, the kernel helps pick the one annotators rated higher, beyond length. Helpfulness AUC 0.571 (length only) → 0.602 (length + kernel). Coherence 0.486 → 0.571: length says nothing about coherence, the kernel does.
  • Much of that signal is repetition. θ (1 − word overlap between neighbouring sentences) reads low when an answer repeats itself. A direct count of repeated 4-word phrases does better than the kernel on coherence (0.615). With length and repetition both in, the kernel still adds a small amount for helpfulness, correctness and coherence (CIs above 0).
  • Pooling across prompts mixes two things. A model fitted across all answers gains held-out signal (helpfulness +0.038), but it does not pick the better answer within a pair any better than length. dₛ against complexity flips sign: -0.030 pooled, +0.053 within pair.
  • Verbosity is length (pair AUC 0.975 from length alone; the kernel adds nothing).
  • Two v19 operating-window notes. θ = 0 means "one segment", not "perfectly smooth": 262 eligible answers (1.4%) were read as a single sentence (lists without bullet markers, numbered lists on one line). And θ works as an indirect repetition reader.

1 · Question and data

Do v19's axes line up with what human annotators scored, once length is accounted for? HelpSteer2 (NVIDIA, CC-BY-4.0): 21,362 responses, two per prompt, five human scores 0-4. The kernel was frozen before it saw this data; annotators never saw the kernel. Length is the shared confound and is controlled in every analysis.

Eligible after the reading frame: 19,333 responses across 10,334 prompts; 8,999 prompts have both answers eligible. Excluded (reasons can overlap): short_prose 1,899, low_english_function_words 148, no_letters 78, non_latin_script 18.

Score distributions known before reading: coherence sits at the ceiling (15,298 of 21,362 score 4); complexity is mostly 1-2.

Step 3 · What θ carries (added analysis G)

θ had the largest within-pair weight for helpfulness and coherence, in the direction opposite to P4. θ is 1 − mean word cosine between adjacent sentences, so low θ means neighbouring sentences reuse words. Second bearing: share of repeated 4-word phrases, counted outside the kernel. Same text, different measurement, so the two are not fully independent.

4 · Operating windows for v19

  • θ = 0 is a segmentation state. v19 returns θ = 0 for any text read as one sentence. Lists without bullet markers and numbered lists on a single line land there, with very high Δy.
  • θ is an indirect repetition reader. It tracks repeated phrasing (ρ = -0.44 with a 4-gram count); a direct count reads repetition better.
  • Pooled vs within-prompt readings differ. dₛ and complexity change sign. Cross-prompt studies need prompt-relative reading (v19's prompt-family baselines) before axis claims.
  • Raw vs framed made no difference here; only 240 of 21,362 responses have code fences.
