Reward-model audit on Arena 2024 battles

Lab book · 2026-11-11 · frozen v19 through reading frame r1.1 · same 5,794 battles as the judge run · predictions frozen in PLAN.md before any reward model ran (two amendments, both recorded before analysis)

Findings
  • Reward models keep rewarding structure where voters stop. Between two answers already in the 2024 style, voters give the structure index nothing (+0.002); ArmoRM gives +0.413, GPM +0.422, PairRM +0.244, all with intervals above zero. Their weight is as large or larger there than between unstyled answers. The Claude judge sits in between (+0.075).
  • They penalise repetition far more than voters, at least ten times more relative to length (10 to 47 times across models and cohorts; intervals wide, all excluding zero).
  • Not self-preference, not code. Dropping battles involving ArmoRM's own student model, or all code battles, leaves the result in place.
  • No trainer fingerprint. Llama-3-8B models tuned on each reward model's preferences moved from their base along a shared direction; ArmoRM's direction (cosine 0.94 with the AlpacaEval judge, 0.89 with HelpSteer2 humans) matched every shift best. The trainer-specific prediction (R6) is not supported.
  • They agree with voters less than the judge does: 61%/58%/53% between unstyled answers (ArmoRM/GPM/PairRM), against the judge's 73%.

1 · Setup

Three open reward models, each used to produce preference data for a styled model on AlpacaEval (model cards): ArmoRM-Llama3-8B-v0.1 (gemma-2-9b-it-SimPO, gemma-2-9b-it-WPO-HB, REBEL-Llama-3-8B-Instruct-Armo), GPM-Gemma-2B (SPPO/GPO-Llama-3-8B-Instruct-GPM-2B), PairRM (SPPO-*-PairRM). Run as published on one rented L40S (Russell's Runpod account), libraries pinned (transformers 4.44.2), inputs up to 4,096 tokens. ArmoRM's first run stopped at battle 393 because token-level truncation removed the chat marker its gating needs; it was rerun from scratch with text-level shortening (armorm: 87 answers shortened to fit 4096 tokens). GPM and PairRM scored both answer orders.
